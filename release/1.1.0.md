Hotbar 1.1.0 adds window arrangement and more ways to customize your macOS switcher.

- Arrange the selected window: left half, right half, maximize, center, or move to the next display.
- Choose the switcher's position and accent color, and show or hide hotbar app names.
- Pin favorite apps to the top and sort windows by recent use, app name, or title.
- Click a saved hotbar slot to switch to its window.
- Correct number-row shortcuts for slots 5–9.
- Show purchase pricing in USD (US$9.99, one-time purchase).

Install by opening the DMG and dragging Hotbar into Applications. Accessibility permission enables switching and arrangement; Screen Recording permission enables optional window previews. Keep one installed copy of Hotbar to avoid conflicting shortcuts and permissions.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Window previews require macOS 14 or later.
